WHAT OFTEN GOES WRONG

Common mistakes we see

01

No Script or Shot List

Shooting without a script or shot list, which wastes time on set and leaves gaps in editing.

02

Wrong Format for the Platform

Ignoring platform-specific formats — a horizontal video shot for YouTube doesn't work as a vertical Instagram reel.

03

Overloaded Edits

Overloading edits with unnecessary transitions and effects that distract from the actual message.
