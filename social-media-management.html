WHAT WE DO

More than a posting schedule

Social media management isn't just posting a few graphics every week. Done properly, it's a connected workflow: an audit of where your accounts stand today, a strategy built around your actual audience, a content calendar that turns that strategy into a plan, creative production that brings each post to life, publishing on a consistent schedule, community management so comments and DMs get a real response, and a performance review that feeds into next month's plan.

That's the difference between an account that posts occasionally and one that's actually managed. We run this process for restaurants, local service businesses, D2C and fashion brands, and clinics in Lucknow and across India — building a content strategy suited to how each specific audience actually behaves on Instagram and Facebook, rather than applying the same template to every brand.

WHAT WE CREATE

Content formats, and when each one earns its place

Reels

Best for reach and discovery — new-audience-facing content, trends, and anything worth watching to the end.

Static Posts

Good for product shots, announcements, and anything that needs to sit cleanly in a grid without motion.

Carousels

Useful for anything that benefits from a few slides — tips, before/after, or explaining something step by step.

Stories

Best for day-to-day updates, polls, and behind-the-scenes moments that don't need to live permanently on the grid.

Promotional Content

Offers, launches, and anything with a direct commercial message — used deliberately, not on every post.

Educational Content

Tips, how-tos, and answers to questions your audience is actually asking — builds trust between the sales posts.

Product & Service Content

Content that showcases what you actually sell or offer, styled to fit the platform rather than repurposed straight from a catalogue.

Festival & Occasion Content

Timely posts around relevant festivals and occasions that keep the account feeling current and connected to your audience's calendar.

SOCIAL MEDIA GROWTH

How organic growth actually works

We won't promise a follower count or "viral" growth — nobody can guarantee that honestly. What we can do is build the conditions that make organic growth more likely.

That means understanding your audience properly, keeping content consistent instead of sporadic, testing which formats and hooks your specific audience responds to, staying current with platform trends and hashtag strategy, and reviewing performance regularly instead of guessing. Growth also depends on things outside content alone — your offer, your existing audience, your industry, and how active your competitors are. We're upfront about that instead of promising a number we can't control.

COMMUNITY MANAGEMENT

Management means more than publishing

A lot of "social media management" stops at scheduling posts. We treat comments and DMs as part of the job — responding to audience interaction, keeping an eye on recurring questions people ask, and flagging important enquiries so they don't sit unanswered in an inbox. That ongoing back-and-forth is what actually separates content creation from social media management.

ANALYTICS & REPORTING

Reporting that leads to decisions, not just a PDF

Each month, we review reach, engagement, follower growth, and which content formats and topics actually performed — then use those patterns to shape next month's content plan. The goal is a strategy that keeps adjusting based on what your specific audience responds to, not a static plan run on autopilot with a report attached at the end.

WHAT OFTEN GOES WRONG

Common mistakes we see

01

Posting Without a Plan

Posting inconsistently for a few weeks, then going quiet the moment things get busy — consistency matters more than any single post.

02

Every Post Is a Pitch

Treating every post as a sales pitch, with no content that simply builds relationship and trust.

03

Ignoring Comments & DMs

Ignoring comments and DMs, which hurts both engagement and how the algorithm treats your account.

04

Chasing Follower Count

Focusing on follower count alone instead of engagement and enquiries — a smaller, engaged audience is usually worth more than a large, inactive one.

05

Never Reviewing Performance

Posting month after month without checking what's actually working, so the same underperforming approach keeps repeating.
