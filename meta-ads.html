WHAT OFTEN GOES WRONG

Common mistakes we see

01

No Clear Objective

Launching a campaign with no clear objective or defined audience, so the algorithm has nothing useful to optimize toward.

02

Only One Ad Creative

Running only one ad creative and never testing alternatives, which caps how well a campaign can perform.

03

Turning Campaigns Off Too Soon

Turning campaigns off within a day or two, before Meta's algorithm has had time to find the right audience.
