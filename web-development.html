WHAT OFTEN GOES WRONG

Common mistakes we see

01

Design Over Speed

Prioritising design over load speed — a beautiful site that takes 8 seconds to load loses visitors before they see it.

02

No Clear Call-to-Action

No clear call-to-action on the homepage, leaving visitors unsure what to do next.

03

Skipping Device Testing

Skipping real device testing, so the site looks fine on a laptop but breaks on an actual phone.
